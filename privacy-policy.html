Privacy Policy

Quiet Reader · Last updated: November 21, 2026

Quiet Reader is designed to be a completely offline, distraction-free reading application. We respect your privacy and do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

1. Data Collection

Quiet Reader does not collect any personal information, analytics, telemetry, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or usage data. There are no user accounts, cloud sync services, or third-party tracking SDKs.

2. Local Storage & Files

Books and documents you choose to import or scan via Android's system file/folder picker (Storage Access Framework) are copied to the app's private local storage on your device. Reading progress, pinned quotes, categories, display preferences, and reading duration statistics are stored strictly in a local SQLite database on your device and never leave your device.

3. Permissions

Quiet Reader accesses only the specific files or folders you explicitly select through the system picker. It does not request broad device storage access, location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

4. Children's Privacy

Because Quiet Reader collects no data whatsoever, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
